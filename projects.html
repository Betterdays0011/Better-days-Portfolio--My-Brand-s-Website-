Better Days Education in detail

The organisation I founded, explained in full: why it exists, what makes it different, what it offers and where it is heading.

Our vision

Many students and teens struggle academically, and even those who perform well often lack essential life skills, confidence, clarity of purpose and future-ready abilities. Better Days Education bridges this gap by equipping teens with academic excellence, purpose discovery and transformative skills for a stable, successful future.

Our mission

To raise a new generation of teens who graduate secondary school with clarity, strong values, purpose, practical life skills and future-ready competencies, including tech skills that enhance financial stability.

What makes us unique

Our vision is in line with SDG 4 and the African Union’s Agenda 2063 (Aspiration 1, Goal 2). We address deep-rooted challenges that traditional schooling often ignores.

  • Learn about the SDGs
  • Learn about Agenda 2063
  • Lack of purpose and direction
  • Low self-esteem or an inferiority complex
  • Limited critical thinking
  • Absence of real-world and financial skills
  • Academic struggle without tailored support

What we do

We offer online and offline lessons, purpose and life-skills teaching, and development programmes for teens and for schools.
